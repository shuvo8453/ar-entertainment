FAQs about Theme Song & Brand Anthem Production in Bangladesh

A brand theme song is a commissioned musical composition created specifically to represent a brand, organisation, event, or cause. Unlike a jingle — which is a short, catchy advertising hook — a brand theme song is a fuller, more substantive musical piece designed to convey brand values, evoke emotion, and build long-term brand identity. Theme songs are used across corporate events, brand films, institutional communication, development sector campaigns, and social awareness productions.

A jingle is a short, memorable advertising hook — typically 5 to 30 seconds — designed to stick in the listener's memory and drive product recall. A theme song is a longer, more emotionally resonant musical piece — typically 1 to 3 minutes — created for a specific brand, event, series, or campaign. A brand anthem is the most comprehensive of the three — a full musical and audiovisual expression of a brand's purpose, values, and identity, often used for brand launches, anniversaries, or institutional communication. Libanza Films produces all three, with lyrics development, music composition, recording, and full audiovisual production under one roof.

Theme song production costs in Bangladesh typically range from BDT 1.5 lakh for a simple vocal-and-instrument composition through to BDT 8 lakh or more for a full brand anthem with professional recording, arrangement, mixing, mastering, and an accompanying brand film. Cost depends on musical complexity, number of vocalists, studio recording sessions, lyric development requirements, and whether a visual production is included. Libanza Films provides a detailed budget after reviewing your brief.
