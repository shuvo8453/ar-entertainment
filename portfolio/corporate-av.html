FAQs about Corporate AV Production in Bangladesh

Corporate AV (Audiovisual) production is the creation of video content for corporate communication — including company profiles, brand films, institutional documentaries, product launch videos, CSR films, event coverage, and internal communications. Unlike TVCs or OVCs which are advertising-focused, Corporate AV is designed to build trust, explain vision, showcase capability, and communicate brand values to stakeholders, clients, investors, and partners.

Libanza Films produces the full range of Corporate AV — company profiles, brand films, real estate AV, technology company films, institutional AV for NGOs and development organisations, CSR films, product launch videos, event documentation, and corporate documentary films. Clients include Gardenia, Coppertech, Automation Cable, Automation Engineering, Bashundhara Housing, Nextblock, Green Banasree, and Green Model Town.

Corporate AV production budgets in Bangladesh typically range from BDT 1.5 lakh for a single-day interview-based company profile to BDT 10 lakh or more for full multi-location brand films with professional crew, motion graphics, and post-production. Cost depends on shoot days, locations, scripting complexity, talent, motion graphics requirements, and deliverable formats. Libanza Films provides a detailed budget after reviewing your brief.

A standard Corporate AV production at Libanza Films takes 2 to 5 weeks from brief to final delivery — covering scripting, pre-production planning, filming, editing, motion graphics, sound design, and review rounds. Simpler company profile videos can be delivered in 2 to 3 weeks. Complex multi-location brand films may require 5 to 8 weeks depending on scope.

A TVC (Television Commercial) is a short advertising film — 30 to 60 seconds — designed to drive brand awareness or product sales through broadcast television. A Corporate AV is a longer, more substantive video — typically 2 to 10 minutes — designed to communicate a company's story, values, capability, or vision to a specific stakeholder audience such as clients, investors, partners, or employees. TVCs persuade; Corporate AVs inform, build trust, and establish credibility.
