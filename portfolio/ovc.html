FAQs about Online Video Commercial Production in Bangladesh

An Online Video Commercial (OVC) is a brand video produced specifically for digital platforms — Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok — rather than broadcast television. OVCs are optimised for the platform's format, audience behaviour, and algorithm requirements. They are typically shorter, hook-first, and designed to perform in a mobile-first, sound-off environment.

A TVC (TV Commercial) is produced for broadcast television and typically runs 30–60 seconds at broadcast quality. An OVC is produced for digital platforms, often in multiple formats (16:9, 1:1, 9:16), with shorter durations (6–30 seconds) and platform-native hooks. TVCs require broadcast-grade production; OVCs require platform-native storytelling. Libanza Films produces both.

OVC production budgets in Bangladesh typically range from BDT 70,000 for straightforward social content to BDT 400,000+ for full branded productions with casting, location, and post-production. The final cost depends on concept complexity, shoot days, talent, locations, and number of output formats. See our OVC Cost Guide for a detailed breakdown.

Libanza Films produces OVCs for Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. Each platform has different format requirements, audience behaviour, and algorithm preferences. We produce platform-specific versions — 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1 and 4:5 for Facebook feed, 9:16 for Reels, Stories, and TikTok — so every OVC performs in its native environment.
