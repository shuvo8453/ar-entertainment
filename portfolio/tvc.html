FAQs about TV Commercial Production in Bangladesh

TV Commercial production is the end-to-end process of creating a television advertisement — from concept development, scriptwriting, casting, and location scouting through to filming, editing, sound design, colour grading, and broadcast-ready delivery. A TVC is designed for television channels and follows broadcast quality standards set by the respective broadcaster.

TVC production budgets in Bangladesh typically range from BDT 2 lakh for simpler single-location productions to BDT 15 lakh or more for multi-location, multi-day shoots with professional talent and full post-production. The final cost depends on concept complexity, shoot days, cast, locations, special effects, and post-production requirements. Libanza Films provides detailed budgets after a brief review.

A standard TVC production at Libanza Films takes 3 to 6 weeks from brief to broadcast-ready delivery — covering concept development, scriptwriting, pre-production planning, casting, location scouting, filming, editing, and post-production. Complex productions with multiple locations or talent may require additional time. Rush timelines can be accommodated depending on scope.

A TVC (Television Commercial) is produced for broadcast on TV channels — it follows broadcast quality specifications, runs 30 to 60 seconds, and is designed for a passive, lean-back viewing experience. An OVC (Online Video Commercial) is produced for digital platforms — Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok — with platform-native formats, shorter hooks, and multiple aspect ratios. Libanza Films produces both, with strategic planning tailored to each channel.
