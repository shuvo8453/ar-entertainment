Film Fixer Services for International Productions

When a documentary crew, broadcaster or NGO team arrives in Bangladesh, the single most important decision they make is who they hire as their local film fixer. Libanza Films provides filming permits, journalist and media visas, location access, local crew and equipment, and on-ground logistics so your team can focus on the story instead of navigating Bangladesh from overseas.

Every international production is overseen personally by Azizul Hoque Shiplu, Founder and Film Director, with experience in film production since 2007 and direct oversight of productions for clients from the USA, UK, Belgium, Romania, Malaysia, Ecuador, Singapore, UAE, France and India, including Al Arabiya News (UAE broadcast), Joshua Chronicles (USA documentary) and multiple NGO and development organisation productions across Dhaka, Cox's Bazar, Sundarbans, Sylhet and Chittagong.
