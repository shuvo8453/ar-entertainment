What affects OVC cost the most in Bangladesh?

Online video commercial costs vary widely because production requirements differ from campaign to campaign. Understanding these cost drivers helps you plan a realistic budget and avoid unnecessary expenses.

  • Duration & deliverables: 6s / 10s / 15s / 30s videos, plus cutdowns and aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 16:9)
  • Creative complexity: Simple demo vs scripted storytelling with multiple scenes
  • Locations: Single-controlled location vs multi-location logistics
  • Talent & casting: In-house presenters vs professional actors
  • Art direction: Props, set styling, wardrobe, makeup
  • Post-production: Motion graphics, sound design, colour grading, VFX
  • Timeline: Tight deadlines may require additional crew or parallel workflows

Who should invest in an Online Video Commercial?

OVCs work best when you already know what you want to achieve - awareness, leads, or conversions. If any of the following describe your situation, an online video commercial is a strong investment.

You should invest in OVC if:
  • You are running Facebook or YouTube ads regularly
  • Your static ads are underperforming
  • You want higher engagement and conversion rates
  • You are launching a new product or offer
  • You want scalable ad creatives with multiple cutdowns
You may need a different format if:
  • You want a long-form brand or corporate story
  • The video is primarily for internal or stakeholder use
  • You need a documentary-style narrative

In those cases, consider Corporate AV production or Documentary filmmaking.

Bottom-line: If performance, reach, and measurable results matter, OVC is designed for exactly that.
