OVC Production for Banks & Financial Services in Bangladesh

Why do banks and financial brands in Bangladesh need OVC?

Bangladesh's financial sector is one of the most competitive advertising categories on digital platforms. With over 60 commercial banks, multiple MFS providers and a rapidly expanding insurance and fintech sector, professional online video commercials (OVC) are now the primary tool for financial brands to acquire customers, build trust and drive digital adoption across Facebook, YouTube and Instagram.

Financial brands face a unique challenge in advertising: they need to build trust quickly, communicate clearly and operate within strict compliance boundaries — all while competing for attention in one of the most crowded digital advertising environments in Bangladesh.

At Libanza Films, we specialise in producing online video commercials for banks, MFS brands, insurance companies, NBFIs and fintech startups in Bangladesh. Our team brings together financial brand communication expertise, compliance-aware scripting and platform-optimised production to deliver OVCs that perform where it matters — in the Facebook feed, on YouTube pre-roll and inside digital banking platforms.

Whether you are launching a new loan product, promoting digital banking adoption, running a festive campaign or building institutional brand equity, we produce video that communicates credibility and drives action.

OVC Use Cases for Banks & Financial Brands in Bangladesh

Financial brands in Bangladesh invest heavily in OVC across multiple campaign objectives throughout the year. Here are the most common OVC use cases we produce for banks and financial services clients:

Product Launch OVC

Every new financial product — a loan, deposit scheme, credit card, insurance plan or investment product — needs a strong launch OVC to drive awareness and trial. We produce product launch videos that communicate features, benefits and eligibility clearly without compromising engagement or brand tone.

  • Personal & SME loan campaigns: Feature-led OVC with real customer benefit storytelling.
  • Deposit & savings products: Trust-led visual language with strong call-to-action.
  • Credit card launches: Lifestyle-oriented OVC showing card benefits in context.
  • Insurance product awareness: Emotional, scenario-driven storytelling to drive consideration.

Digital Banking & MFS Adoption Campaigns

Driving app downloads, account registrations and transaction volume through digital banking and mobile financial service OVC. We produce mobile-first video content that makes complex onboarding feel simple and relevant to Bangladesh's diverse banking audience.

  • Internet banking adoption OVC: Demonstrating ease-of-use and security for first-time digital users.
  • MFS onboarding campaigns: Relatable, localised storytelling for mass-market MFS uptake.
  • App feature highlight videos: Short-form OVC showcasing specific features like bill pay, transfers and QR payments.
  • Security & fraud awareness: Compliance-driven content that protects customers and builds institutional trust.

Festive Campaigns & CSR Communication

Eid, Puja and national holiday campaigns represent the highest-spend periods in Bangladesh's financial advertising calendar. We help banks and financial brands communicate seasonal relevance while building emotional connection with their audience.

  • Eid & Puja brand OVC: Emotion-led storytelling that connects financial products to the spirit of celebration.
  • CSR film production: Documentary-style or narrative OVC that communicates your social impact credibly.
  • Institutional brand campaigns: Long-form brand films and shorter OVC cutdowns for multi-platform distribution.
  • Anniversary & milestone communications: Celebrating your brand journey with your customers and stakeholders.

Insurance & NBFI Video Commercials

Insurance and non-bank financial institutions in Bangladesh face a particular communication challenge: explaining complex products to audiences with low financial literacy while building the trust required for long-term commitment decisions. We help insurance brands produce OVCs that simplify, reassure and convert.

  • Life insurance awareness OVC: Scenario-based storytelling that communicates protection and peace of mind.
  • Health insurance product OVC: Benefit-led videos for individual and group health coverage products.
  • Takaful & Islamic finance OVC: Shariah-compliant product communication with appropriate visual language.
  • Microfinance & NBFI campaigns: Empowerment-led narrative OVC for rural and underserved audience segments.

Performance & Direct Response OVC

Not every financial OVC is brand-building. Many campaigns need to drive specific actions — loan applications, account openings, card sign-ups or app downloads. We produce performance-focused OVC specifically engineered for direct response on Facebook and Google.

  • Lead generation OVC: Strong hook, clear benefit, specific CTA — built for Facebook lead forms and landing pages.
  • Retargeting ad videos: Short reminder-format OVC for audiences already familiar with your product.
  • A/B testing variants: Multiple hook and CTA versions from a single production to support creative testing.
  • YouTube pre-roll OVC: 6-second bumper and 15-second skippable formats for YouTube campaign delivery.

Financial Sectors We Serve

Libanza Films produces OVC for the full spectrum of financial services organisations in Bangladesh:

  • Commercial Banks — local and international banks operating in Bangladesh
  • Islamic Banks — Shariah-compliant product communication and brand campaigns
  • Mobile Financial Services (MFS) — bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay and other MFS providers
  • Insurance Companies — life, general, health and takaful brands
  • Non-Bank Financial Institutions (NBFIs) — leasing, factoring and investment companies
  • Microfinance Institutions (MFIs) — BRAC, Grameen and other MFI brands
  • FinTech & Digital Payment Brands — new-generation payment and lending apps
  • Stock Brokerages & Asset Management — capital market and investment product communication

What Makes Financial Services OVC Different

Producing OVC for a bank or financial brand is meaningfully different from standard consumer brand production. Three principles guide everything we do for financial services clients:

Trust is the product.
In financial advertising, the brand itself is what's being sold — not just the product. Visual language, talent selection, colour palette and narrative tone all carry enormous weight. We script and shoot financial OVC with a deep understanding of what builds credibility versus what undermines it.

Compliance cannot be an afterthought.
Financial product claims, interest rates, terms and disclosures must be handled accurately. Our scriptwriting process incorporates your compliance team's requirements from the brief stage — not after production — avoiding costly reshoots or approval delays.

Platform behaviour is different for financial content.
Bangladesh's financial audience on Facebook skews older, more urban and more deliberate in their attention patterns than FMCG consumers. We plan OVC format, duration and hook strategy accordingly — including longer-form pre-roll for complex product explanations and short-form scroll-stopper formats for awareness campaigns.

Our OVC Production Process for Financial Brands

  • 1. Brief & Compliance Review: We begin by understanding your product, campaign objective, target audience and any regulatory or legal communication requirements specific to your financial product category in Bangladesh.
  • 2. Strategy & Script Development: Our creative team develops a platform-specific creative strategy, script and visual direction that communicates trust, clarity and brand authority — the three pillars of effective financial OVC.
  • 3. Talent & Production Planning: We cast appropriate talent that reflects your brand's customer profile, select locations and plan the shoot for maximum efficiency and compliance alignment.
  • 4. Production: We execute a professional shoot — studio, banking hall, office or lifestyle location — with precise direction and visual quality standards appropriate to a regulated financial brand.
  • 5. Post-Production & Compliance Sign-Off: We edit, colour grade and finalise the OVC. All legal disclaimers, brand compliance and mandatory disclosures are incorporated cleanly before you send for final approval.
  • 6. Platform Delivery: Final files delivered in all required formats and aspect ratios — 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 — for Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, OTT and digital banking app placements.

OVC Production Cost for Financial Brands in Bangladesh

How much does OVC production cost for a bank in Bangladesh?

OVC production for financial brands in Bangladesh typically starts from BDT 150,000 for focused product launch videos and ranges to BDT 2,000,000+ for full institutional campaigns with multiple scenes, professional talent, high-volume cutdowns and multi-platform delivery. Key cost drivers include campaign complexity, talent, locations, number of final versions and post-production requirements.

Financial OVC production in Bangladesh is typically priced at a slight premium to standard consumer brand OVC due to three factors: the compliance review layer added to pre-production, the higher production standards expected of regulated financial institutions, and the multi-version delivery requirements of most financial media plans.
