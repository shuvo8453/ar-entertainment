OVC for Brands & FMCG in Bangladesh

Why do FMCG brands invest most heavily in OVC in Bangladesh?

FMCG brands in Bangladesh now invest more in online video commercial (OVC) production than in any other media format — because Facebook and Instagram deliver reach at a fraction of TVC cost, with measurable sales impact. At Libanza Films, we specialise in FMCG OVC production that combines advertising agency creative strategy with professional video production — trusted by PRAN-RFL, Bombay Sweets, Olympic Industries and more.

FMCG brands are the largest investors in online video commercials (OVC) in Bangladesh. From product launches to always-on performance ads, OVC drives awareness, trial, and repeat purchase across Facebook, YouTube, and short-video platforms — at a speed and cost that TVC simply cannot match.

At Libanza Films, we help FMCG brands create OVCs that combine memorable storytelling with measurable platform performance. Our founding director Azizul Hoque Shiplu, who began his advertising career at Ogilvy & Mather, leads creative direction on every FMCG brief — bringing advertising agency strategic discipline to every production.

We have produced OVC campaigns for PRAN-RFL Group (KnockOut, Swift, Joom, Wonder Cake, All Time, Ring Chips), Bombay Sweets & Co. Ltd. (Ring Chips, Party Chips), Olympic Industries (Energy Plus, Pulse Candy), and Teer — City Group.

Why FMCG brands in Bangladesh rely on OVC

In Bangladesh's mobile-first marketplace, FMCG brands compete for consumer attention on Facebook, YouTube and Instagram every single day. OVC gives FMCG marketers the speed, scale, and targeting precision that no other media format can deliver at comparable cost.

  • High competition at the digital shelf — consumers encounter multiple competing FMCG brands in a single scroll. Strong OVC creative wins attention before a competitor does
  • Short consumer attention spans — the first 1–2 seconds of an FMCG OVC determines whether a consumer watches or scrolls. Production quality and creative hook are everything
  • Frequent variant and offer launches — FMCG brands need new OVC content constantly. Libanza Films shoots for multi-asset delivery, reducing cost per creative significantly
  • Platform-first buying behaviour — Bangladesh consumers discover and decide on FMCG products on Facebook and YouTube. OVC meets them at the point of influence
  • Measurable, performance-driven ROI — unlike TVC, FMCG OVC delivers cost-per-reach, frequency, and conversion data that brand managers can act on immediately

FMCG OVC Use Cases

Each FMCG OVC we produce is built around a specific business objective — not just a format requirement. Here are the most common FMCG OVC briefs Libanza Films delivers:

Product Launch OVC

Introduce new SKUs, flavours, pack sizes or variants with high-impact launch OVCs built for maximum awareness in the first 72 hours. We produce Facebook, Instagram and YouTube versions from a single shoot.

Offer & Promotion OVC

Time-sensitive discounts, bundles, Eid promotions, trade deals and festive campaigns. These OVCs are built for speed — concept to delivery in days when required — without sacrificing production standard.

Always-On Performance Ads

Short 6–15 second videos built for retargeting, remarketing and sustained brand recall. Designed to run at high frequency without fatigue — multiple creative hooks produced from a single shoot.

Brand Storytelling OVC

Longer-form 30–60 second OVCs that build brand equity, communicate brand values, or position a product in consumers' lives. Suited for YouTube and Facebook Watch placements with higher-intent audiences.

Consumer Testimonial OVC

Authentic consumer-led endorsements for FMCG categories where trust and social proof drive purchase. We produce testimonial OVCs with professional lighting, direction and post-production.

AI FMCG Brand Video

For brands needing high-frequency content at lower cost, we produce AI-generated FMCG brand videos — all human-directed and brand-supervised. Already delivered for PRAN-RFL and other FMCG clients. Learn more →

Platform-First FMCG OVC Strategy

Different platforms require different creative structures. Libanza Films builds FMCG OVCs around platform behaviour — not just brand aesthetics — ensuring every asset is optimised for the format, duration, and algorithm it will run on.

Facebook & Instagram (Meta)

The primary FMCG OVC platform in Bangladesh. We produce Meta-first OVCs with a 1–2 second hook before the skip threshold, optimised for silent viewing with on-screen text, and delivered in 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 formats for feed, stories and reels simultaneously.

  • 1–2 second hook required
  • 6s / 10s / 15s format options
  • Square and vertical versions included
  • High-frequency delivery optimised

YouTube

YouTube pre-roll allows for stronger FMCG brand storytelling with 15–30 second formats. We produce skippable and non-skippable YouTube OVCs with brand lift objectives — building purchase intent and category association beyond the immediate click.

  • 15–30 second storytelling format
  • Skippable and non-skippable versions
  • Brand lift campaign optimised
  • Connects to TVC for integrated campaigns

Frequently Asked Questions — FMCG OVC

FMCG OVC is short-form digital video advertising produced specifically for platforms like Facebook, YouTube and Instagram. For FMCG brands in Bangladesh, OVC is the single most effective way to build rapid brand recall, drive product trial at scale, and stay competitive in a market where consumers spend more time on mobile platforms than any other media. Unlike TVC, FMCG OVC can be tested, optimised and scaled quickly — making it ideal for product launches, always-on campaigns and seasonal promotions.

Facebook and Instagram (Meta) deliver the highest reach for FMCG brands in Bangladesh, driven by over 50 million active users and very low CPM rates. YouTube is effective for longer storytelling, brand lift campaigns and pre-roll placements. TikTok and short-video formats are growing rapidly for younger consumer segments. Libanza Films produces FMCG OVCs in all required formats — 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 — for optimal performance across all placements.

For Facebook and Instagram, 6–15 seconds is the optimal duration for FMCG performance ads — with a strong hook in the first 1–2 seconds before the skip option appears. For YouTube pre-roll, 15–30 seconds allows for stronger product storytelling and brand recall. Libanza Films produces all required durations and aspect ratios from a single shoot, maximising creative ROI across every placement.

FMCG OVC production costs in Bangladesh depend on creative scope, number of product variants, cast, locations and the number of final format versions required. A full-scale FMCG campaign production with professional cast, multiple locations and multi-platform delivery ranges from BDT 250,000 to BDT 800,000 or more.
