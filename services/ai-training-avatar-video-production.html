AI Training & Avatar Video Production

Human-Led AI Training Videos & Digital Avatars for Global Organisations

Libanza Films delivers human-led, AI-assisted training and avatar video production for enterprises, government bodies, NGOs, and multinational organisations. These services are part of our broader AI Video Content Creation capability.

We specialise in producing scalable, consistent, and compliant training content with AI avatars, multilingual voice delivery, and structured instructional design. Professional human supervision ensures accuracy, cultural appropriateness, and brand safety.

Our AI training solutions support HR, Learning & Development (L&D), compliance, onboarding, and internal communications. They enable organisations to standardise knowledge delivery across regions while controlling production costs.

How Much Does AI Avatar Training Video Production Cost?

A single AI avatar training video typically starts from BDT 35,000, with a full enterprise training module starting from BDT 85,000. Multilingual rollout and large training libraries are quoted based on volume and language count. Most single modules are delivered within 5-10 working days.

Why Organisations Choose AI Training & Avatar Videos

Built for High-Volume Enterprise Training

Traditional training videos are costly, slow to update, and difficult to scale. AI-assisted training videos enable organisations to efficiently produce and maintain large volumes of content without sacrificing clarity or control.

Designed for Procurement & Compliance Teams

Our workflows align with enterprise procurement expectations:

  • Clear scope and repeatable formats
  • Version control and update flexibility
  • Disclosure-ready AI usage
  • GDPR-aware production processes

Low Creative Resistance, High Adoption

Unlike advertising content, training videos prioritise clarity, consistency, and accuracy. AI avatars are well-suited for delivering structured communication.

What Is AI Training & Avatar Video Production?

AI training and avatar video production uses AI-generated presenters (avatars) to deliver structured instructional content, supported by human-reviewed scripts, pronunciation checks, and quality control.

These videos are especially effective for:

  • Policy communication
  • SOP walkthroughs
  • Compliance and regulatory training
  • Employee onboarding
  • Knowledge standardisation across teams

Libanza Films uses a human-in-the-loop production model, unlike automated or self-service avatar tools. Every output is professionally reviewed before delivery.

AI Avatar-Led Training Videos

Suitable for:

SOP, Compliance & Policy Communication Videos

This is especially valuable for:

  • Regulated industries
  • Large workforce environments
  • Distributed teams

Videos are designed for clarity, consistency, and audit-ready delivery.

SCORM-Ready & LMS-Compatible Delivery

Training videos can be delivered in formats compatible with:

  • SCORM / xAPI frameworks
  • Internal portals and HR platforms

We support structured modules, segmented lessons, and easy updates.

How Our AI Training Production Workflow Works

1. Training Strategy & Script Control

We begin by reviewing:

  • Training objectives
  • Target audience
  • Language and region requirements
  • Compliance considerations

Scripts are structured for clarity, learning flow, and accuracy.

2. AI Avatar & Voice Generation

AI tools are used to generate:

  • Avatar presenters
  • Voice delivery
  • Initial video drafts

This stage focuses on speed and scalability rather than final approval.

3. Human Review & Quality Assurance

Our team reviews:

  • Content accuracy
  • Pronunciation and pacing
  • Subtitle clarity
  • Training suitability

Corrections are applied before final approval.

4. Localisation, Formatting & Delivery

Final outputs are prepared for:

  • LMS platforms
  • Internal portals
  • Regional deployment
  • Future updates and revisions
