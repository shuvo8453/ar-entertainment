What is Video Description?

Video description is a written narration that explains key visual elements of a video — helping viewers clearly understand what’s happening on screen. Descriptions are added during natural pauses in dialogues to explain:

  • Actions and gestures
  • Facial expressions and emotions
  • Background details
  • Important visual elements

Why is Video Description Important?

Effective video descriptions enhance accessibility, improve engagement, and support compliance across digital and broadcast platforms.

  • Wider audience reach
  • Improved brand image
  • Stronger accessibility compliance
  • Higher engagement
  • Support for multitasking users
  • Great for educational content
  • Competitive digital advantage
  • Inclusion for visually impaired audiences
