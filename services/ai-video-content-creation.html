What Is AI Video Content Creation?

AI video content creation is the process of producing video — explainer videos, promotional clips, training modules, social media ads and corporate AV — using artificial intelligence tools for script development, AI voice generation, AI avatars or presenters, visual generation and automated editing, combined with human creative direction and review at every stage.

At Libanza Films, AI video content creation in Bangladesh pairs these AI tools with human scriptwriters, editors and AI specialists who review every output for brand accuracy, Bangla and English language quality, and originality before delivery — so AI speeds up production without removing the human judgement that makes a video actually work for your audience.
