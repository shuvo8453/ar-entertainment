Online Video Commercial (OVC) Services in Bangladesh

What is an online video commercial (OVC) in Bangladesh?

An online video commercial (OVC) is a short professionally produced video advertisement created for digital platforms — Facebook, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. In Bangladesh, OVC is now the dominant brand advertising format, replacing a significant share of TVC investment as mobile internet reaches over 120 million users. Our OVC Production Process

Every Libanza Films OVC follows a structured, discipline-driven production process that protects quality, timelines and budget across every project size.

  • 1. Brief & Consultation: We begin by understanding your brand objective, target audience, platform strategy and campaign goals. A clear brief leads to a sharper, faster production.
  • 2. Planning & Research: Our creative team develops the script, visual direction and storyboard based on your brief, audience behaviour and platform requirements.
  • 3. Production: We execute the shoot — studio or on-location across Bangladesh — with professional cameras, lighting and sound. Every frame is captured with the final edit in mind.
  • 4. Editing & Post-Production: Our editors craft the story, apply colour grading, sound design, motion graphics and produce all required format versions.
  • 5. Platform Delivery: All final OVC files are delivered in the correct specs — 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 — for Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and other placements.
