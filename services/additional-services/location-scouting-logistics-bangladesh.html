Location Scouting & Logistics in Bangladesh

Professional Location Research, Access & Production Logistics for International Shoots

Libanza Films provides professional location scouting and production logistics services in Bangladesh for international filmmakers, documentary teams, broadcasters, advertising agencies, NGOs, and foreign production houses.

Bangladesh offers a rare combination of dense megacities, rivers, ports, rural landscapes, factories, coastal areas, and heritage sites. Identifying the right locations and filming them legally requires deep local expertise.

As a trusted local partner, we help international crews plan locations, secure access, and manage logistics so production runs smoothly from arrival to wrap.

Who Needs Location Scouting in Bangladesh?

  • International documentary filmmakers
  • Foreign directors, producers and cinematographers
  • TV broadcasters and streaming platforms
  • Advertising agencies and branded content teams
  • NGOs, INGOs and development organisations
  • Journalists and investigative reporting teams

For foreign crews, professional location scouting is essential for accuracy, access, and compliance.

Why Location Scouting in Bangladesh Requires Local Expertise

  • Restricted or sensitive locations
  • Local authority permissions
  • Traffic and transport limitations
  • Weather, seasonal and daylight variables
  • Cultural and political sensitivities

Libanza Films bridges creative vision with regulatory and logistical reality.

Our Location Scouting Services in Bangladesh

Location Research & Creative Matching

  • Urban, rural, coastal, and industrial locations
  • Heritage sites, markets and neighbourhoods
  • Ports, factories, infrastructure, and institutions

Location Access & Permissions

  • Local authority and property owner permissions
  • Police and administrative liaison
  • Sensitive and restricted area coordination

Production Logistics Planning

  • Transport routing and scheduling
  • Accommodation and crew movement planning
  • Equipment transport coordination
