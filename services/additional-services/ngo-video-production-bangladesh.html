NGO Video Production in Bangladesh for Development Organisations and INGOs

Bangladesh is one of the most important development stories in the world. With over 170 million people, a climate-vulnerable delta geography, rapid urbanisation and one of the world's most active NGO sectors, Bangladesh is consistently at the centre of international development communication — and the organisations working here need a production partner who understands both the storytelling and the sector.

Libanza Films provides specialist NGO video production services in Bangladesh for international NGOs, INGOs, UN agencies, bilateral donors and development-focused foundations. We produce impact stories, donor communication films, programme documentation, health and social development content, advocacy films and annual reporting video — with the ethical rigour and production quality that the development sector demands.

We are not a general production house that occasionally works with NGOs. Development sector communication is a core part of what we do. Our teams understand beneficiary dignity, safeguarding obligations, community consent processes, donor reporting formats and the narrative standards that distinguish genuine impact storytelling from extractive portrayals of poverty and vulnerability.

  • Experienced in filming with vulnerable populations, children, health patients and climate-displaced communities
  • Safeguarding-compliant production with informed consent protocols and child protection procedures
  • Full bilingual capability — Bangla for local distribution, English for international donor audiences
  • Delivery in multiple formats: impact shorts, programme overviews, full documentaries and donor-portal-ready files

Development Organisations We Produce For

Bangladesh hosts one of the most diverse development sectors in the world. From the world's largest NGO (BRAC) to the country offices of major UN agencies, bilateral donors and hundreds of international humanitarian organisations, the demand for high-quality development communication content is constant, significant and growing.

Libanza Films provides NGO video production services for:

  • International NGOs and INGOs: Care International, Save the Children, Oxfam, World Vision, ActionAid and similar organisations operating in Bangladesh who need field-produced impact content for global communications teams.
  • United Nations agencies: UNICEF, UNDP, WHO, WFP, UNFPA, UNHCR and other UN bodies with Bangladesh country offices requiring programme documentation and advocacy films.
  • Bilateral development donors: Organisations funded by FCDO, USAID, GIZ, Sida, NORAD and EU development programmes requiring donor-reportable video evidence of programme impact.
  • Health-focused NGOs: Organisations working in maternal health, child health, nutrition, vaccination, water and sanitation, and mental health requiring patient and community story content with clinical sensitivity.
  • Climate and environment organisations: Organisations documenting climate displacement, coastal erosion, flood response and environmental change across Bangladesh's most vulnerable communities.
  • Education and livelihood programmes: Development organisations communicating learning outcomes, skills development, women's economic empowerment and youth programme impact to funders and partner organisations.

NGO Video Content We Produce in Bangladesh

Development organisations working in Bangladesh require a wide range of video content types — from two-minute social media impact stories to full-length programme documentaries. Libanza Films delivers all formats, planned and produced to the specific brief, audience and intended distribution channel of each project.

  • Beneficiary impact stories: Human-centred short films (2–5 minutes) following a programme participant's journey — the format most used for donor communication, annual reports and fundraising.
  • Programme documentaries: Long-form (10–30 minutes) documentary films that communicate the scope, methodology and outcomes of a development programme to institutional donors, boards and academic audiences.
  • Field documentation: Systematic footage of programme activities, community settings, health facilities and field operations — used for monitoring, evaluation, learning (MEL) and internal reporting.
  • Donor and funder communication films: High-quality, branded films produced specifically for presentation to major institutional funders, including multi-year grant reports and programme renewal proposals.
  • Behaviour change communication (BCC) content: Video materials designed for community-level health, hygiene, nutrition or safety behaviour change — delivered in Bangla and adapted for target community audiences.
  • Advocacy and campaign films: Urgent, issue-led films for digital and media advocacy campaigns — climate, gender rights, child protection, displacement and humanitarian crisis communications.
  • Training and capacity-building video: Instructional content for NGO field staff, community health workers, teachers and other programme implementers.
  • Annual report and campaign content: Short video clips, highlight reels and motion graphics formatted for digital annual reports, websites and social media channels.

Safeguarding, Consent and Ethical Storytelling

For NGO and development sector productions, how content is made matters as much as what it contains. Libanza Films applies a rigorous ethical production framework on all development sector work — built around the core principles of beneficiary dignity, informed consent, child protection and responsible storytelling.

  • Informed consent processes: All filming involving beneficiaries, community members and health patients is conducted with written and/or verbal informed consent — in Bangla and with independent interpreter confirmation where required.
  • Child protection compliance: All crew working on productions involving children observe strict child protection protocols aligned with international safeguarding standards, including no unsupervised access and parental or guardian consent documentation.
  • Dignity-centred storytelling: Libanza Films does not produce content that portrays beneficiaries as passive recipients of aid or reduces individuals to their hardship. Beneficiaries are filmed as agents of their own stories — with voice, agency and context.
  • Community and institutional permissions: We manage all access permissions at community, facility and institutional level — including health facility approvals, government site permissions and community leader engagement before filming begins.
  • Data sensitivity: Footage and interviews involving sensitive personal disclosures — medical, displacement, protection or trauma-related — are handled with confidentiality protocols agreed with the commissioning organisation in advance.

Where We Film Across Bangladesh

Bangladesh's development challenges — and the NGO programmes addressing them — are spread across dramatically different environments. Libanza Films has the field experience and local networks to film across all of them.

Dhaka — Urban Poverty and Health

Urban slums, community health centres, rehabilitation facilities, schools and programme offices across the capital. Ideal for urban poverty, health system and livelihood programme content.

Cox's Bazar — Rohingya Response

The world's largest refugee settlement. Libanza Films supports INGOs, UN agencies and humanitarian organisations producing content about the Rohingya crisis, camp conditions, response programmes and resilience stories.

Coastal Belt and Delta — Climate Stories

Cyclone-affected coastal communities, river-eroded char islands and salinity-threatened agricultural land — the front line of the global climate crisis and the subject of international climate communication campaigns.

Rural Bangladesh — Community Programmes

Haor wetlands, northern char areas, Barind Tract and rural districts across all eight divisions — where the majority of development programming in health, education, agriculture and WASH takes place.

Sundarbans — Environmental Programmes

The world's largest mangrove forest and a UNESCO World Heritage Site — location for biodiversity, conservation, climate adaptation and community livelihood documentary production.

Sylhet — Tea Gardens and Hill Communities

Tea estate worker communities, indigenous hill tracts populations and biodiversity-rich environments — relevant to fair trade, indigenous rights and environmental programming.

How We Work With NGOs and Development Organisations

Development sector productions operate under tighter timelines, greater logistical complexity and more sensitive ethical requirements than commercial video work. Our production process is designed around these realities — ensuring every project is planned with the care the subject matter and the commissioning organisation require.

  1. Project brief and programme understanding: We begin with a detailed conversation about your programme, your target audience, your donor requirements and the story you need to tell. We read relevant programme documents and understand the theory of change before approaching the creative brief.
  2. Ethical and logistical pre-production: We develop a production plan that covers consent processes, safeguarding protocols, location access permissions, beneficiary selection (in collaboration with your field teams), crew briefings and interview guide preparation.
  3. Field production with your programme team: Where required, we coordinate closely with your field staff for community access and beneficiary facilitation — ensuring filming does not disrupt programme delivery and that participants are genuinely comfortable on camera.
  4. Sensitive post-production: Editing focuses on authentic narrative — we do not impose a generic development video template. Each film is structured around the specific story, the programme logic and the intended audience's emotional and informational needs.
  5. Review, revision and multi-format delivery: We provide a structured review process, incorporating feedback from your communications and programme teams. Final delivery includes all required duration cuts, subtitle versions and format specifications for each distribution channel.
