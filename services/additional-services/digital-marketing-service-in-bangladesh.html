Our Digital Marketing Services

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Boost your website's visibility in Google and other search engines with our comprehensive SEO services:

  • Keyword Research: Identifying high-impact English and Bangla keywords relevant to your business and industry.
  • On-Page SEO: Optimising content, meta tags, headings and internal links to improve rankings.
  • Off-Page SEO: Building quality backlinks and enhancing your website's authority across the web.
  • Technical SEO: Ensuring fast load times, mobile-friendliness, structured data and secure connections.

Social Media Marketing

Engage your audience and build a strong community around your brand on social platforms:

  • Platform Management: Managing Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube pages.
  • Content Creation: Developing engaging static, video and story content that reflects your brand voice.
  • Community Management: Responding to comments, messages and queries to nurture brand loyalty.
  • Social Media Advertising: Running targeted ad campaigns to reach the right audience and drive conversions.

Pay-Per-Click (PPC) Advertising

Maximise your return on ad spend with well-structured and continuously optimised PPC campaigns:

  • Google Ads: Search, Display, YouTube and Performance Max campaigns tailored to your goals.
  • Social Media Ads: Highly targeted campaigns on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.
  • Display Advertising: Visually appealing banner ads designed to capture attention.
  • Campaign Optimisation: Ongoing optimisation of bids, creatives and audiences to improve performance.

Content Marketing

Build authority and trust with educational, helpful and search-friendly content:

  • Content Strategy: Developing a long-term content roadmap aligned with your brand goals.
  • Blog Writing: Creating informative and SEO-optimised blog posts in English and Bangla.
  • Infographics: Designing visually engaging graphics that simplify complex ideas.
  • Video Content: Producing social and brand videos in collaboration with Libanza Films.

Email Marketing

Nurture leads and build loyalty with personalised, well-timed email campaigns:

  • Email Campaign Strategy: Planning newsletters, drip campaigns and promotional sends.
  • List Segmentation: Grouping your audience to deliver relevant and targeted messages.
  • Automated Workflows: Setting up automated journeys for welcome, cart recovery and re-engagement.
  • Performance Analysis: Tracking opens, clicks and conversions to refine future campaigns.

Website Design and Development

Create a modern, fast and user-friendly website that represents your brand and converts visitors:

  • Custom Website Design: Responsive designs that reflect your brand identity and user needs.
  • eCommerce Development: Secure and conversion-focused online stores for products and services.
  • Content Management Systems (CMS): Implementing platforms such as WordPress or custom solutions.
  • Website Maintenance: Ongoing technical support, security and performance optimisation.

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

Turn more visitors into leads and customers with continuous CRO improvements:

  • A/B Testing: Testing variations of landing pages, CTAs and layouts to find top performers.
  • User Experience (UX) Design: Improving navigation, forms and flows for a smoother journey.
  • Data-Driven Insights: Analysing user behaviour via analytics and heatmaps.
  • Landing Page Optimisation: Designing and refining conversion-focused landing pages.

Digital Marketing Cost in Bangladesh

Digital marketing costs in Bangladesh vary depending on the services required, campaign scope and the level of creative production involved. The following provides a general guide to help plan your investment:

  • Social Media Management (2–3 platforms): Monthly retainer starting from BDT 25,000–60,000 depending on content volume and ad spend management.
  • SEO (ongoing): Monthly retainers typically range from BDT 20,000–70,000 based on competition, keyword scope and technical requirements.
  • PPC / Google Ads Management: Agency management fee is separate from ad spend and varies by campaign complexity and monthly budget.
  • Content Marketing (blogs, infographics): Depends on volume, language (English or Bangla) and research depth.
  • Full-Service Digital Marketing Packages: Integrated packages combining SEO, social, content and paid are available as monthly or project-based retainers.

Every engagement begins with a consultation to understand your goals and recommend the right investment for your business. Contact us for a tailored proposal.
