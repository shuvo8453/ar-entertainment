Film Fixer in Dhaka for International Productions

Libanza Films provides professional film fixer services in Dhaka for international productions from USD 2,000 for fixer-only support. Ministry of Information filming permits for Dhaka take 10 to 21 working days. Named Dhaka productions supported include Joshua Chronicles (USA, street documentary), Al Arabiya News (UAE, ongoing broadcast news), and commercial productions for FCB India and international advertising agencies.

Dhaka is the political, economic and cultural heart of Bangladesh. From government institutions and corporate headquarters to dense urban neighbourhoods, rivers, ports, garment factories and rapidly transforming infrastructure, Dhaka offers an unmatched range of real-world visuals for international storytelling. Filming here is complex: heavy population density, layered authority structures, sensitive locations and administrative requirements make it essential to work with a trusted local film fixer. Libanza Films ensures your production remains legal, safe, efficient and uninterrupted.

Why International Productions Film in Dhaka

Visual Diversity in a Single City

Within a short radius, Dhaka offers modern business districts, government institutions, historic Old Dhaka streets, the Sadarghat river port, industrial zones, garment factories and diverse residential neighbourhoods — multiple visual worlds without relocating cities.

Real Stories at Massive Scale

Home to over 20 million people, Dhaka is a city of migration, economic growth, innovation and social contrasts. It is the primary destination for international documentary, investigative journalism, NGO development storytelling and urban studies productions in South Asia.

Gateway to Nationwide Filming

All national authorities, ministry offices, major broadcasters and NGO headquarters are based in Dhaka. For most international productions, Dhaka is the operational entry point for filming across Bangladesh — permits, crew, equipment and logistics all flow from here.

Location-Specific Filming Challenges in Dhaka

  • Severe traffic congestion affecting shoot schedules — early morning windows, alternate route planning and buffer time built into all Libanza Films Dhaka schedules
  • Layered authority structures for sensitive locations — Hazaribagh tanneries, garment factory zones, Sadarghat port and government institutions each require different clearance chains
  • Dense urban filming attracting crowds and public attention — Libanza Films deploys crowd management and police coordination where required
  • Political and editorial sensitivity — certain subject areas, protest zones and government-adjacent filming require specific pre-production context briefing from Libanza Films before the crew arrives
  • Restricted drone airspace across most of central Dhaka — CAAB approval required, 3 to 4 weeks processing after MoI permit
  • Rapidly changing urban environment — Libanza Films conducts pre-shoot location confirmation even for locations previously scouted

Libanza Films anticipates these challenges at the brief stage and plans proactively so they do not become shoot-day problems.
