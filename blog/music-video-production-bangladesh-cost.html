Music Video Production in Bangladesh: Process, Cost and What to Expect in 2026

Music video production in Bangladesh typically costs BDT 1,50,000 to 8,00,000 depending on concept complexity, location count, cast and post-production scope. Libanza Films has produced music videos for domestic artists and commercial brands. This is the first dedicated cost guide to music video production in Bangladesh — because no other production company or media resource in the Bangladesh market has published one.

If you are an artist, a music label, a manager or a brand considering a music video production in Bangladesh — whether for YouTube, Facebook, OTT platforms or broadcast television — this guide covers what a music video actually costs at each level, what the production process involves and what you should expect from a professional production company before you sign anything.

Music Video Production Cost in Bangladesh: The Three Tiers

Tier 1 — Entry-Level Music Video (BDT 1,50,000 – 2,50,000)

Single location or controlled studio, performance-focused (artist singing/playing), one shoot day, standard camera and lighting package, basic colour grade and edit.

  • Pre-production (concept, shot list, location): BDT 20,000–40,000
  • Director: BDT 20,000–40,000
  • Crew (DP, camera assistant, gaffer, PA): BDT 35,000–55,000
  • Equipment (camera, lights, gimbal): BDT 25,000–40,000
  • Location (studio hire or single exterior): BDT 15,000–25,000
  • Styling and makeup: BDT 10,000–20,000
  • Post-production (edit, grade, titles, delivery): BDT 25,000–50,000
  • Total: approximately BDT 1,50,000–2,70,000

Tier 2 — Mid-Range Narrative or Concept Video (BDT 2,50,000 – 5,00,000)

Two to three locations, narrative story elements with additional actors, one to two shoot days, strong art direction and colour palette, professional VFX or graphic treatment, multiple delivery formats.

  • Pre-production (creative direction, storyboard, multiple location scouts): BDT 60,000–1,00,000
  • Director (experienced narrative director): BDT 50,000–1,00,000
  • Crew (full music video team, 2 days): BDT 1,00,000–1,50,000
  • Equipment (cinema camera, prime lenses, full lighting): BDT 70,000–1,20,000
  • Art direction and set dressing: BDT 30,000–60,000
  • Additional cast and wardrobe: BDT 30,000–60,000
  • Post-production (VFX, colour, sound mix, format variants): BDT 80,000–1,50,000
  • Total: approximately BDT 4,20,000–6,40,000

Tier 3 — High-End Production (BDT 5,00,000 – 8,00,000+)

Complex concept, multiple locations including controlled builds, full art department, international-standard cinematography, significant VFX or motion graphics, broadcast and OTT delivery suite.

  • Pre-production (full creative development, production design): BDT 1,50,000+
  • Senior director: BDT 1,50,000+
  • Full professional crew (10+ people, 2–3 days): BDT 2,00,000+
  • Cinema camera and lens package: BDT 1,50,000+
  • Set and location builds, art direction: BDT 1,00,000+
  • Post-production (VFX, 3D, grade, DCP, OTT delivery): BDT 2,00,000+
  • Total: BDT 9,50,000+ at this tier

The Music Video Production Process in Bangladesh

  • Stage 1 — Brief and concept (1–5 days). The artist, label or brand shares the track, the intended platform, the visual reference direction and the budget. The production company presents one to three concept directions for selection. At Libanza Films, this concept presentation is delivered within five working days of receiving the brief.
  • Stage 2 — Pre-production (5–10 days). Once concept is approved: locations are scouted and booked, cast (if any additional actors) is confirmed, styling and wardrobe are prepared, a detailed shot list is built and a production schedule is confirmed. The director reviews the track in detail and marks specific moments in the edit timeline that the visual treatment will correspond to.
  • Stage 3 — The shoot (1–2 days typically). Music video shoots in Bangladesh are almost always lip-synced and performance-recorded on playback. The artist performs to the track through a speaker on set — not live. The production captures performance takes across all planned setups, with directorial choices for energy, angle and expression at each setup. Additional narrative elements (if any) are shot separately or intercut with performance footage.
  • Stage 4 — Post-production (7–14 days). Offline edit: selecting the best performance takes for each section of the track, building the visual rhythm and pacing, integrating narrative footage. Colour grade: establishing the visual palette. VFX (if applicable): motion graphics, digital environments, glitch effects. Sound: the master audio track is used directly — music videos are not re-recorded in post. Final delivery: YouTube master (H.264, 4K or 1080p), social versions (9:16, 1:1), broadcast export if required.

What Drives Music Video Cost in Bangladesh

The biggest cost drivers

  • Concept complexity — a pure performance video in one studio is an order of magnitude cheaper than a narrative concept with multiple locations, actors and set builds
  • Location count — each additional location adds a half-day minimum of shoot time, transport and logistics cost
  • VFX and post-production scope — simple cuts and a colour grade versus complex visual effects and 3D elements
  • Additional cast — actors, background performers, professional dancers each carry day rates and may require casting
  • Art direction and set design — built environments, prop procurement and set dressing add production value but add cost

How to get the most from your budget

  • Commit to a single strong visual concept rather than trying to execute three ideas in one video
  • Choose locations that are visually strong rather than geographically complex — a visually interesting single location beats three mediocre ones
  • Brief the post-production scope in advance — unexpected VFX requests after the shoot add significant unbudgeted cost
  • Allow adequate post-production time — rushed grading and editing shows in the final product and is the most regretted saving in music video production
  • Share platform delivery specifications upfront — the cost of producing a YouTube 4K master and a broadcast version simultaneously is much lower than requesting the broadcast version after final delivery

Brand Music Videos: How Commercial Brands Use Music Video Production in Bangladesh

Brand music videos are a growing format in the Bangladesh market. Commercial brands commission music video-style productions for several purposes: brand anthems for corporate identity, branded entertainment on YouTube and Facebook, product-integrated music content for social platforms, and employee culture or recruitment films styled as music videos.

The production pipeline for a brand music video is identical to an artist music video — the addition of brand elements (product, logo, messaging) is managed in pre-production alongside the creative concept, not added after. Brand music videos at Libanza Films are quoted against the same tier structure as artist productions, with an additional creative development fee for brands requiring original composition.

If you are a brand considering a music video format for your next campaign, this is one of the least utilised and most undercosted formats in Bangladesh advertising — the cost differential between a standard OVC and a brand music video of comparable production quality is smaller than most marketing managers expect.
