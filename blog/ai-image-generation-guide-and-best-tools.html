AI Image Generation Guide (2026): Tools, Prompts & AI Content Expertise

AI image generation has rapidly evolved from experimentation to one of the most powerful visual creation technologies available today. A simple text prompt can now generate production-ready images for TVCs, corporate AVs, social media, design prototypes, mood boards, and campaign KVs—within seconds.

Libanza Films is one of the pioneers in Bangladesh using AI image & video generation for brands, corporates, NGOs, INGOs, and international productions. Supported by Libanza Advertising’s AI Content Development Team and Graphics & Art Team, Libanza ensures culturally accurate, brand-safe, fast, and high-quality AI visuals.

Get a Quotation

1. What Is AI Image Generation?

AI image generation uses diffusion and multimodal models to convert text prompts into original, high-quality visuals. Models like GPT-4o Image, DALL·E, Midjourney, SDXL, and Adobe Firefly understand:

  • Objects & environments
  • Lighting & colour grading
  • Camera style, lens, composition
  • Art direction & mood

OpenAI’s latest models are fully multimodal—able to understand text, images, and context together for superior results.

2. Why AI Image Generation Matters for SEO, AI Search & AEO

The search ecosystem is shifting dramatically:

  • AI Overviews now answer visually
  • Image-based answers rank higher
  • Users ask conversational queries like:
    “Show a cinematic Dhaka office scene with natural daylight.”

To stay searchable:

  • Use descriptive filenames: dhaka-office-ai-visual-libanza.webp
  • Write meaningful ALT tags describing every visual
  • Include Q&A patterns inside your content for AEO
  • Maintain brand consistency across all AI visuals

Libanza Films + Libanza Advertising combine human art direction with AI tools, ensuring results that are accurate, brand-safe, and SEO-friendly.

  • Clarify mood & purpose

Step 2 — Use a Structured Prompt

Template:

[Subject] + [Action] + [Environment] + [Lighting] + [Mood] + [Camera] + [Negative Prompt]

5. Writing Better Prompts

Weak vs Improved Prompts

Weak: “Bangladeshi office”

Improved:
“Bangladeshi corporate office with glass partitions, professionals brainstorming, cinematic daylight, 16:9, high realism.”

Reusable Prompt Templates
