Video Production Portfolio | Libanza Films Bangladesh

Libanza Films is the video production arm of Libanza Limited, a 360° brand communication agency founded in 2016. Our founding director Azizul Hoque Shiplu began his career at Ogilvy & Mather and is a graduate of the Zahir Raihan Film Institute. Our production portfolio spans 200+ TV commercials, online video commercials, corporate AV, documentaries, brand theme songs, and AI brand videos.

Clients represented across our portfolio include Bashundhara City, Bashundhara Housing, CSRM, Astha Life Insurance, Bombay Sweets, PRAN-RFL, Akij Ceramics, Akij Cement, ECO+, Teer, LG, Concord-Fantasy Kingdom, Coppertech Industries, Gardenia, Nextblock, IFAD Group, Nitol Motors — Tata Commercial Vehicles, Aman Cement, BIR Cement, Amin Mohammad City, Canadian University of Bangladesh, BSEZ, MK Electronics, DGFP, HPNSP, Olympic Industries, and many more.

Every production — whether a broadcast TVC, a corporate brand film, a social impact documentary, or an AI brand video — begins with the same strategic discipline: understand the brand, define the audience, build the idea, then execute with precision and creative ambition.

Why Libanza Films?

At Libanza Films, production capability and creative strategy are inseparable. Our founding director Azizul Hoque Shiplu brings Ogilvy advertising thinking and Zahir Raihan Film Institute cinematographic training to every brief. Co-founder Monirul Islam Masum brings visual storytelling experience from BBC Media, ETV, and Maasranga TV, with brand production credits across Unilever, Nestlé, Reckitt Benckiser, Berger, and PRAN-RFL.
