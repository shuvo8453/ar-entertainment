How Libanza Films Works with US Clients

Our remote-first production model is built around the realities of US-to-Bangladesh collaboration. Most US clients find the workflow efficient and predictable after their first project.

1. Brief & Scope

You send us your creative brief, brand guidelines, target audience and platform requirements. We respond with a detailed scope, timeline and project estimate — typically within 24 hours.

2. Script & Concept Approval

We develop script, voice direction and visual concept for your approval before production begins. Your legal or compliance team can review at this stage — common for US enterprise clients.

3. Production & Review

We produce and deliver a review draft for your feedback. EST evening sends become Bangladesh morning actions — most projects complete 2–3 review cycles within a standard US business week.

4. Final Delivery

Final files delivered via shared drive in all required formats — 16:9, 1:1, 9:16, closed captions, LMS-compatible formats — ready for your media plan or platform deployment.

Time Zone: US East Coast & Bangladesh

Bangladesh Standard Time (BST, UTC+6) is 10–11 hours ahead of EST. US East Coast evening sends (5–9pm EST) become Bangladesh morning actions (6–10am the next day). This asynchronous rhythm means US-to-Bangladesh projects typically run at the same pace as US-to-West-Coast collaboration — with next-day turnarounds on standard review cycles.
