NZ Compliance, Privacy & Cultural Considerations

New Zealand has its own regulatory and cultural context for AI content production that we take seriously:

  • Privacy Act 2020: We do not use identifiable personal data in AI training or generation workflows. Data minimisation and purpose limitation principles are applied to all NZ client projects.
  • ASA (Advertising Standards Authority NZ): Marketing content for NZ audiences is developed with awareness of ASA guidelines, including requirements around truthful representation and non-deceptive AI-generated content.
  • Health and Safety at Work Act compliance: Workplace training content is produced with HSWA compliance requirements in mind — clear hazard communication, accurate procedural information and appropriate safety messaging.
  • ACC workplace safety standards: We produce ACC-aligned workplace injury prevention and early intervention content for NZ employers.
  • Māori cultural sensitivity: For content involving Māori contexts, te reo Māori or tikanga references, we work closely with your cultural advisors and recommend NZ-based Māori cultural review for all such content.
  • Human review gate: Every deliverable is reviewed and approved by experienced human creatives before delivery. No automated output leaves our workflow without professional sign-off.

How Libanza Films Works with New Zealand Clients

Our remote-first production model is built for efficient NZ-to-Bangladesh collaboration. The time zone relationship between New Zealand and Bangladesh creates a natural asynchronous workflow that most NZ clients find fast and predictable.

1. Brief & Scope

You send your creative brief, compliance requirements and brand guidelines. We respond with a detailed scope, timeline and project estimate — typically within one NZ business day.

2. Script & Concept Approval

We develop script, voice direction and concept for your approval. Cultural or compliance review can be completed at this stage before production begins.

3. Production & Review

Review drafts delivered for feedback. NZ afternoon sends (3–5pm NZST) become Bangladesh early morning actions — most projects complete 2–3 review rounds within a standard NZ work week.

4. Final Delivery

Final files delivered via shared drive in all required formats — 16:9, 1:1, 9:16, SCORM, closed captions — ready for LMS deployment, broadcast or digital distribution.

Time Zone: New Zealand & Bangladesh

New Zealand Standard Time (NZST, UTC+12) is 6 hours ahead of Bangladesh Standard Time (BST, UTC+6). NZ afternoon sends (3–5pm NZST) become Bangladesh early morning actions the following day — enabling same-day NZ turnarounds on most standard review cycles. For NZ clients this feels similar to working with an Auckland studio with a morning production shift.
