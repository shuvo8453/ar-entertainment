Australian AI Ethics Framework & Content Compliance

Australia's approach to AI governance is principles-based and sector-led rather than a prescriptive regulatory regime. Libanza Films builds relevant compliance into every Australian client project:

  • Australian AI Ethics Framework: Our human-in-the-loop production model directly addresses the framework's 8 principles — including human oversight, transparency, accountability, accuracy and fairness. Government departments and publicly-funded organisations can request documentation confirming alignment.
  • Australian Consumer Law (ACL): All advertising and commercial content is produced with ACL non-deception and substantiation requirements — no misleading claims, accurate product and service representations.
  • ASIC guidance for financial content: Financial services video produced with awareness of ASIC regulatory guidance on financial promotions, product disclosure and conduct obligations.
  • TGA-sensitive healthcare content: Therapeutic goods, pharmaceutical and healthcare content produced with awareness of TGA advertising requirements for Australia.
  • Privacy Act 1988: Data minimisation and purpose limitation principles applied to Australian project data handling. Personal data is not used in AI training or generation workflows.
  • Accessibility (DDA compliance): Closed captions and audio description tracks available as standard for publicly distributed educational and government content.
