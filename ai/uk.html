ASA/CAP Compliance & UK GDPR-Aware Production

The UK has clear and established regulatory standards for AI content in advertising and business communication. Libanza Films builds these into the production workflow for every UK client project:

  • ASA/CAP advertising standards: We apply CAP Code requirements to all UK advertising content — no misleading claims, accurate product representations, and appropriate AI disclosure labelling where required by current ASA guidance.
  • AI disclosure: Where ASA guidance or your organisation's policy requires disclosure of AI-generated content, we incorporate compliant disclosure language as part of the production workflow, not as a post-delivery addition.
  • UK GDPR compliance: Data minimisation, purpose limitation and transparency principles applied to all UK project data handling. No personal data is used in AI training or generation without documented lawful basis.
  • ICO accountability standards: Production documentation available to support accountability obligations under UK GDPR where required by enterprise or public sector clients.
  • FCA regulated content awareness: Financial services advertising produced with awareness of FCA conduct requirements for financial promotions.
  • Human review gate: Every deliverable is reviewed and approved by experienced human creatives before delivery. No automated output leaves our workflow.

Review drafts delivered for your feedback. UK morning sends become Bangladesh afternoon actions — most UK projects complete 2–3 review rounds within a standard UK work week.

Final files in all required formats — 16:9, 1:1, 9:16, closed captions, SCORM — ready for your media plan, LMS deployment or platform distribution.

Time Zone: UK & Bangladesh

UK time (GMT, UTC+0 / BST, UTC+1) is 5–6 hours behind Bangladesh Standard Time (BST, UTC+6). UK morning sends (8–10am GMT) become Bangladesh afternoon actions — enabling same-day UK turnarounds on most review cycles. Most UK clients describe the workflow as equivalent to working with a UK studio with a flexible delivery team.
