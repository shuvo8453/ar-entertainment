UAE Cultural Standards & Content Compliance

Producing content for UAE audiences requires a specific and non-negotiable understanding of cultural, religious and regulatory standards. Libanza Films applies these standards across all UAE client work:

  • NMC (National Media Council) standards: Content for UAE audiences is produced with awareness of NMC content guidelines — no material that contradicts UAE laws, values or social norms.
  • Islamic content sensitivity: All UAE content respects Islamic values — modest visual representation, halal-appropriate product portrayal, avoidance of content inappropriate under UAE standards.
  • Arabic language accuracy: All Arabic AI dubbing and text content is reviewed by Arabic language specialists for grammatical accuracy, appropriate dialect selection and cultural tone.
  • Khaliji vs MSA distinction: We distinguish between Modern Standard Arabic for formal institutional content and Khaliji Gulf dialect for consumer-facing and brand communication.
  • Ramadan and seasonal sensitivity: Content calendared around UAE religious and national occasions is planned with appropriate tone, timing and cultural alignment.
  • UAE Vision 2031 alignment: Institutional content reflects the aspirational, progressive and internationally positioned brand of the UAE as outlined in government vision documents.
