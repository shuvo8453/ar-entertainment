Why Europe Benefits from a Single Production Strategy

EU AI Act — One Regulation, 27 Markets

The EU AI Act applies uniformly across all 27 EU member states — the same Article 50 disclosure requirements, the same transparency obligations for AI-generated synthetic speech and imagery, and the same risk-classification framework. Organisations producing content for the EU do not need country-specific AI compliance solutions: one EU AI Act-compliant workflow covers the entire internal market.

GDPR — Universal European Data Standard

GDPR applies across all 27 EU member states and the EEA (including Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein). One GDPR-compliant data processing framework for AI content production covers the entire European market. Libanza Films provides GDPR-compliant DPAs for all European enterprise clients and applies data minimisation and transparency principles across every European project.

One English Master → 24 EU Official Languages

The EU has 24 official languages. Multinational organisations and EU institutions producing content for European-wide distribution need multilingual delivery at scale. Libanza Films produces a single English master and localises into French, German, Italian, Spanish, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Czech and other EU languages simultaneously — all from one brief, one production investment, with human specialist review in each language.

EU AI Act, GDPR & European Compliance Framework

The EU has the world's most comprehensive AI regulatory framework. Libanza Films builds EU compliance into every European production workflow:

  • EU AI Act Article 50 — Transparency requirements: AI-generated synthetic speech, AI-manipulated video likenesses and AI-generated images in content likely to be mistaken for authentic must be disclosed. Libanza Films provides Article 50 disclosure documentation, synthetic content metadata watermarking and transparency records for all EU distribution.
  • GDPR — Data protection: No personal data — voice recordings, images, natural person likenesses — is incorporated into AI generation pipelines without documented lawful basis. GDPR-compliant DPAs available for all European enterprise and public sector clients.
  • Country-specific advertising self-regulation: France ARPP, Germany Werberat, Italy IAP, Netherlands SRC, Sweden RO, Spain AUTOCONTROL — national advertising self-regulatory codes are applied per country for advertising content. National language specialist reviewers apply each country's requirements.
  • Sector-specific EU regulation: MiFID II for financial services advertising, EMA guidance for pharmaceutical promotion, EU MDR for medical device marketing — sector compliance reviewed by human specialists at the output stage.
  • Human approval gate: No European content — in any language, in any sector — is approved for distribution without formal human review and documented sign-off. AI accelerates production; humans remain accountable for every EU deliverable.
